# Greenlight — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

Greenlight is a private tool used by a ticket-buying team to coordinate cart
approvals between buyers and their manager. This policy explains what
information the extension handles and where it goes.

## What the extension handles

- **Your settings** — the display name and role you choose, and the team access
  key you enter. These are stored locally in your browser.
- **Cart details** — when you add tickets to a cart on a supported ticketing
  site, the extension reads the cart's public details (event name, date, venue,
  section/row/seat, quantity, price, and the on-page seat map) so it can be
  reviewed by your manager.

## Where it goes

Cart details and your chosen display name are sent **only to your team's own
private backend service**, so your manager can approve or deny the cart and the
result can be recorded for your team. Nothing is sent to any other destination.

## What we do NOT collect

- No passwords or payment/financial information.
- No browsing history or pages outside the supported ticketing sites.
- No keystroke, mouse, or behavioral tracking.
- No health, location, or advertising data.

## Sharing

We do **not** sell or transfer your data to third parties. Data is used only for
the extension's single purpose — coordinating cart approvals within your team —
and never for creditworthiness, lending, or any unrelated purpose.

## Storage & control

Your settings stay in your browser and are removed if you uninstall the
extension or clear its data. Cart records are held in your team's own backend
and are controlled by your team's administrator.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to your team's Greenlight
administrator.
